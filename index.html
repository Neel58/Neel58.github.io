About

A little about me

I got into ML the way most people do — tutorials, copy-pasted code, models that worked without me fully knowing why. What changed things was hitting a wall I couldn't tutorial my way past, which sent me to the original papers and documentation instead. That's still how I learn: pick something I don't fully understand, rebuild it from scratch, and see where my mental model breaks.

That's why most of the projects here are implemented from scratch rather than fine-tuned off the shelf — the Transformer in BhashaSetu, the U-Net in the solar segmentation pipeline. I'd rather spend longer and actually understand what I shipped.

Projects

Selected work

A few things I've built, from a live Kaggle segmentation pipeline to a from-scratch transformer.

01

Solar filament segmentation

Kaggle notebook ↗

End-to-end image segmentation pipeline for a live Kaggle competition (MAGFiLO 2026), converting 1,154 annotated solar images into 18,464 leak-free tiles via COCO-polygon-to-mask conversion and overlapping-tile full-resolution inference. Trained a from-scratch PyTorch U-Net with combined BCE + Dice loss, reaching 0.6414 validation Dice / 0.4815 validation IoU. Competition is still open — pipeline under active tuning.

Python PyTorch U-Net
02

AerialScene — aerial object detection

Kaggle notebook ↗

Object-detection pipeline on the iSAID aerial imagery dataset, in active refinement. Implements overlap-aware tiling with a leak-free split, hard-negative mining, and a transfer-learning Faster R-CNN (ResNet50-FPN) detector evaluated under real COCO-protocol mAP, plus a from-scratch full-image inference pipeline with cross-tile NMS merging to reconstruct detections across tile boundaries.

Python PyTorch torchvision
03

BhashaSetu — English → Gujarati NMT

github.com/Neel58/BhashaSetu ↗

From-scratch Transformer encoder-decoder (2 blocks, 8-head attention, ~13M params) for English-to-Gujarati translation, trained on 1.5M sentence pairs within a 9-hour GPU budget. Reached 87.5% token-level validation accuracy by epoch 2 of 5 using mixed-precision training and the warmup/inverse-sqrt LR schedule from "Attention Is All You Need." Shipped as a live Streamlit demo with greedy and beam-search decoding.

Python TensorFlow / Keras Streamlit
Live demo ↗
04

Voltrix — power consumption forecasting

github.com/Neel58/Voltrix ↗

Next-hour power-consumption forecasting pipeline on the UCI Household Electric Power Consumption dataset, benchmarking naive baselines against XGBoost and a GRU sequence model across a 48-hour input window. Diagnosed near-collinear predictor features through targeted ablation studies, isolating genuine forecasting signal from leakage-prone correlated sensors.

Python XGBoost TensorFlow / Keras
